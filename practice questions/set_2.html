Scenario Brief

Meridian Insights is a boutique market-intelligence firm producing ~120 client reports a month for 40 enterprise customers. Its product is Atlas, a research system on the Agent SDK: a coordinator that decomposes each client question and delegates to four subagents, web_research, document_analysis, data_synthesis, and report_writer. Subagents run in isolated contexts and report back to the coordinator, which aggregates and resolves gaps. Deep-dive analyses are prototyped as named Claude Code sessions. Targets: citation accuracy ≥98% (currently 91%), full coverage of every requested dimension, turnaround under 15 minutes.
